- The preview deployment is protected by Vercel authentication. It is reviewable by signed-in project users, but is not a public-crawlability check.

The work is not a zero-defect guarantee. These gates describe the known remaining gaps and the scope that was actually verified at the time. The production status below supersedes the preview-only state recorded above.
