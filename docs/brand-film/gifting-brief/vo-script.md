# Voiceover Script — My Gifting Brief

**Accent / delivery (Phase 2):** Warm, expressive, professional — conversational Indian English.  
**Feel:** Modern brand advertisement, not a slow software tutorial.  
**Timing:** Sync to visuals; adjust scene durations slightly if needed to finish within ~75 seconds.

---

## Full narration

Meet someone with a big task: welcoming a whole new team.

A hundred and fifty people. One special occasion. And plenty of little details to figure out.

What should go in their welcome gifts? What works for the budget? And how do you bring it all together?

That's where Monkcraft makes gifting easier.

Start by exploring thoughtful gift ideas, from everyday essentials to beautifully curated merchandise.

Found something you love? Save it straight to your Gifting Brief.

Choose your favourites, imagine the perfect gift kit, and add the details that matter.

Your occasion. Your quantities. Your budget. Your branding. Your packaging and delivery preferences.

Everything comes together in one organized place, so you can review your plan and make changes as your ideas take shape.

And when you're happy with your brief, you're ready to request a quotation or discuss your gifting plans with Monkcraft.

Less juggling. More thoughtful gifting.

Monkcraft Gifting Co. Gifting, made joyful.
