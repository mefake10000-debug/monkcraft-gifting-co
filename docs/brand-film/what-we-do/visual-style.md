# Visual Style Notes — Revised Animatic V2

## Look

Photoreal-inspired stylized 3D merch + adult professional characters.  
Signature motion: **sketch → dimensional product** with steel, copper, leather, knit, and packaging materials.

## Benchmarks (principles only)

Swag/NotReal craft bar + Hustlemad Behance colour energy — never copy assets, characters, or transitions.

## Palette

`#662F90` `#3F3F37` `#F1F2F2` + mist lilac, sage mint, powder blue, warm apricot, buttercream, dusty rose.

## Typography feel

Fraunces display · DM Sans UI/labels.
