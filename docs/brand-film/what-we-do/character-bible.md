# Character Bible — Original Monkcraft (Adult Professional)

**Status:** Pre-production design sheets  
**Rule:** Original Monkcraft characters only. Do **not** copy Hustlemad Behance characters, Swag/NotReal models, or childish mascot proportions.

---

## Style

- Adult proportions (realistic head-to-body ratio ~1:7–1:7.5)  
- Natural gestures; expressive but restrained faces  
- Professional-casual wardrobe in Monkcraft pastels + olive neutrals  
- Skin tones inclusive; India-forward casting feel without stereotypes  
- Render: polished stylized adult cartoon / high-end commercial 3D character — **not** baby clay, chibi, or generic mascot

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** Adult hands interacting with real merch silhouettes; soft studio contact shadows.  
**Don’t:** Oversized heads, chubby toddler limbs, megaphone mascots, neon scribble props from Behance refs, or competitor character likeness.
