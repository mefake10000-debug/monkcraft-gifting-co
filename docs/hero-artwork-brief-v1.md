## Approved direction (summary)

Create / revise **one immersive, original hero illustration** for Monkcraft as a **premium commercial gifting hero banner**:

1. **Product-first composition** — curated merchandise and gifting presentation lead  
2. **Character-second** — a refined 3D/cartoon commercial helper presenting/opening the Monkcraft gift box  
3. Clean **left-side type space** preserved for headline and CTAs  

Tone: **playful, colourful, polished, brand-forward, sophisticated.**  
Inspired by colourful, polished corporate gifting campaigns (Hustlemad-level craft).  
**Do not copy** Hustlemad characters, layouts, or proprietary artwork.

Hero copy context (layout only — do not bake into art unless requested):

- Headline: **Gifting, made joyful.**  
- CTAs in type pocket: Explore Gifts · Book a Gifting Consultation  

### Revision focus (current edit pass)

- **Keep** overall composition, layout, product balance, and premium commercial gifting feel  
- **Change** the human figure to a more **cartoon-styled / illustrated** refined commercial character (not realistic)  
- Face, hair, hands, and body: more stylized, simplified, cleanly shaped  
- Preserve warm, friendly expression and the action of presenting/opening the gift box

## 1. Artwork goal

The hero illustration must:

- Read as a **premium Monkcraft corporate gifting hero banner**  
- Showcase **curated merchandise and gifting solutions** clearly  
- Feel playful, colourful, polished, and brand-forward  
- Keep a **clean left-side type space** for UI copy and CTAs  
- Position the character as a **sophisticated brand mascot/helper** in a high-end campaign — never the whole story alone  
- Feel like a talented brand artist made it **specifically for Monkcraft**

Success: product-led showcase first; warm character second; calm type pocket always usable.

---

## 2. Composition direction

### Must

- **One unified studio-like hero scene** with shared lighting and soft integrated pastel environment  
- Immersive illustrated zone (~**65–75%** of desktop hero) — not a cramped media column  
- **Clean left-side type space** (calm cream / light clearing) for headline + both CTAs  
- **Strong balance** between products, packaging, and gifting presentation  
- Character interacts with the **gift box** (presenting / opening) without blocking the type pocket  
- Product arrangement stays intentional and commercial — not messy floating clutter  

### Must not

- Disconnected random floaters or decorative overload  
- Magical / fantasy atmosphere  
- Generic AI clutter or extra invented products beyond the locked set  
- Hard “stock thumbnail” card framing that fights the banner feel  

### Suggested spatial intent (desktop)

| Zone | Role |
|------|------|
| Left type space | Clean, light, uncluttered — logo/nav + headline + CTAs |
| Right / main stage | Product-led gifting showcase + character helper + soft pastel environment |

---

## 3. Character direction

### Concept

Refined **3D/cartoon commercial character** — illustrated and stylized, **not realistic**.  
Adult, modern, stylish, professional, premium.  
Sophisticated **brand mascot/helper** for a high-end corporate gifting campaign.

### Styling (revision)

- More **cartoon-styled and illustrated** than photoreal  
- Face, hair, hands, body: **simplified, clean shapes**, soft stylization  
- Warm, friendly expression retained  
- Action retained: **presenting / opening the Monkcraft gift box**  
- Character-second: supports the product story; does not dominate like a toy mascot  

### Avoid in character

- Childish styling  
- Pixar-like / big-eye fantasy cuteness  
- Overly cute or babyish proportions  
- Generic random mascot energy  
- Hustlemad character lookalikes  

### Role in the scene

Helper/host of the gift reveal — guides attention to the gift box and curated merch; leaves left type space clear.

---

## 4. Product & gifting composition (locked set)

### Product-first rule

Composition is **premium product-led**. Keep the current balance of merchandise, packaging, and gifting presentation.

### Locked items to keep

Retain these elements in the scene (same roles / balance as the current approved composition):

- Black hoodie  
- Black polo  
- Black cap  
- Backpack  
- Bottles  
- Tech organizer pouch  
- Gift box (Monkcraft gifting focal — presenting/opening action)  
- Pen  

### Allowed supporting treatment

- Soft packaging / presentation context around the gift box  
- Clean studio arrangement; polished commercial showcase look  

### Forbidden

- Adding **fake extra products** beyond this locked set  
- Photoreal catalogue cutouts that break the illustrated world  
- Copied Hustlemad motif sets or characters  
- Messy floating element spam  

**Note:** Catalogue pages may use real Monkcraft product photography later. This hero remains a **polished illustrated commercial showcase** of the locked merch set.
