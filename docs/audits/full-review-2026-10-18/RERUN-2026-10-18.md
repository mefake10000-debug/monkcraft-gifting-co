## Verification

- 177 automated tests passed.
- ESLint, TypeScript, catalogue validation, product-description validation, colour-family validation, design-family validation, and the optimized production build passed.
- 64 route/viewport combinations were checked in fresh Chromium contexts: 16 customer routes at 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 px. No document overflow, browser runtime errors, failed media/image responses, or missing page headings were found.
- Catalogue search, sorting, pagination, browser Back, and a real add-to-box action were exercised. The saved line retained `MGC-0481` and its exact product variant.
- Company Store manager setup and the embedded employee preview were opened on mobile and desktop; the employee product detail and return-to-store path stayed inside the presentation.
- The open localhost tab was refreshed and its current navigation and logo links were checked.
- The active logo README was corrected to the actual 55-mark strip and exact brand-filter destinations.

## Still open

- The live Vercel `/api/enquiry` readiness endpoint currently returns `storage_not_configured`. A quote submission cannot be saved until durable storage is configured. Email and WhatsApp are still user-initiated handoffs; neither is an automated delivery integration.
- Five source products still lack exact verified photos: `MGC-2027`, `MGC-2041`, `MGC-4078`, `MGC-4255`, and `MGC-4257`. They remain marked unavailable. (The reviewed `MGC-1422` overlay and provenance need rights confirmation.)
- Production analytics, social profile URLs, physical-device testing, accessibility certification, and customer interviews were not available in this audit.
